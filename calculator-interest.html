Straight Answers

Interest calculator FAQs.

Simple interest is calculated only on the original principal for the entire tenure. Compound interest is calculated on the principal plus any interest already added, so your money earns interest on interest — the more frequent the compounding, the faster it grows.

For the same nominal rate, more frequent compounding (monthly > quarterly > half-yearly > yearly) results in a marginally higher effective return, since interest is added back to principal sooner.
