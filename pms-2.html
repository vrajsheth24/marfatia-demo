01 — Understand It

What PMS is — in one minute

Under the SEBI (Portfolio Managers) Regulations, 2020, a portfolio manager invests directly on your behalf: stocks are bought in your demat account, so you always see exactly what you own. No pooled units — the portfolio is yours, the strategy is tailored, the manager personally answerable to you.

PMS is designed to provide total end-to-end customised and personalised services to HNI and institutional clients, enhancing wealth and returns through expert active fund management to fulfil their financial objectives.

02 — Why PMS

Investing is a full-time job.

Efficient investment management requires time, knowledge, understanding, expertise and constant monitoring of developments in the micro and macro economic environment.

That is difficult for investors because of involvement in their own business, profession and other activities. An experienced fund manager considers your financial goal and the market environment to form the right investment strategy — one that is best suitable for your portfolio.

Given the unpredictable nature of the markets, our fund manager's experience and skill set — along with strong research backup — make your investment grow over the period and help you achieve your financial goals.
