Vision & Mission

Vision: To follow the highest standards of ethics and compliance while facilitating trading by clients in securities in a fair and transparent manner, contributing to the creation of wealth for investors.

Mission:

  • To provide high-quality and dependable service through innovation, capacity enhancement, and the use of technology.
  • To establish and maintain a relationship of trust and ethics with investors.
  • To observe the highest standards of compliance and transparency.
  • To always keep 'protection of investors' interest' as the goal while providing service.
  • To ensure confidentiality of information shared by investors unless required to discharge legal obligations, or investors have provided specific consent to share it.

Services Provided to Investors

  • Execution of trades on behalf of investors.
  • Issuance of Contract Notes.
  • Issuance of intimations regarding margin due payments.
  • Facilitate execution of early pay-in obligation instructions.
  • Settlement of client's funds.
  • Issuance of retention statement of funds at the time of settlement.
  • Risk management systems to mitigate operational and market risk.
  • Facilitate client profile changes in the system as instructed by the client.
  • Information sharing with the client regarding relevant Market Infrastructure Institutions (MII) circulars.
  • Provide a copy of the Rights & Obligations document to the client.
  • Communicating Most Important Terms and Conditions (MITC) to the client.
  • Redressal of investor grievances.

Rights of Investors

  • Ask for and receive information from us about the work history and background of the person handling your account, as well as information about the firm itself.
  • Receive complete information about the risks, obligations, and costs of any investment before investing.
  • Receive recommendations consistent with your financial needs and investment objectives.
  • Receive a copy of all completed account forms and agreements.
  • Receive account statements that are accurate and understandable.
  • Understand the terms and conditions of transactions you undertake.
  • Access your funds in a prescribed manner and receive information about any restrictions or limitations on access.
  • Receive complete information about maintenance or service charges, transaction or redemption fees, and penalties in the form of a tariff sheet.
  • Discuss your grievances with our compliance officer / compliance team / dedicated grievance redressal team and receive prompt, fair attention.
  • Close your zero-balance accounts online with minimal documentation.
  • Get copies of all policies (including MITC) related to dealings on your account.
  • Not be discriminated against in terms of services offered to equivalent clients.
  • Get only advertisement materials that adhere to Code of Advertisement norms in place.
  • In case of broker default, be compensated from the Exchange Investor Protection Fund as per the norms in place.
  • Trade in derivatives after submission of relevant financial documents, subject to our adequate due diligence.
  • Get warnings on the trading system while placing orders in securities under surveillance measures.
  • Get access to products and services in a suitable manner even if differently abled.
  • Get access to educational materials of the MIIs and brokers.
  • Get access to all exchanges of a segment you wish to deal with, unless opted out specifically.
  • Deal with one or more stockbrokers of your choice without any compulsion of minimum business.
  • Have access to the escalation matrix for communication with us.
  • Not be bound by any clause we prescribe that contravenes Regulatory provisions.

Grievance Redressal Mechanism

1

Approach the Stock Broker

Reach out to us at our designated Investor Grievance e-mail ID. We aim to resolve every grievance within 21 days of receiving the complaint. Write to compliance@marfatia.net.

2

Approach the Stock Exchange

If unresolved, escalate to the Stock Exchange. Details on submitting a complaint are available on the respective exchange's website.

3

Approach SEBI (SCORES)

Complaints not redressed at broker/exchange level may be lodged with SEBI's centralized web-based grievance system: scores.gov.in.

Handling of Claims in Case of Broker Default

If a Trading/Clearing Member is declared a defaulter, the Stock Exchange:

  • Issues a circular informing the declaration of the Stock Broker as a Defaulter.
  • Disseminates information about the defaulter on the Stock Exchange website.
  • Issues a Public Notice inviting claims within a specified period.
  • Intimates clients of the defaulter via email and SMS to facilitate lodging claims.

The Exchange website additionally publishes norms for IPF compensation eligibility, the claim form, an FAQ on claims processing, an online claim-status checker, and the SOP for handling investor claims in cases of broker default.
