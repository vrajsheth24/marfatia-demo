Vision & Mission

Vision: To implement diligently researched, customized investment strategies which help investors meet their long-term financial goals in a risk-appropriate manner.

Mission: To ensure that the PMS industry provides a viable investment avenue for wealth creation by adopting high levels of skill, integrity, transparency and accountability.

Details of Business Transacted

  • Appropriate risk profiling of investors.
  • Providing the Disclosure Document to investors.
  • Executing the PMS agreement.
  • Making investment decisions on behalf of investors (discretionary), or at the discretion of the investor (non-discretionary), or advising investors regarding their investment decisions (advisory), as the case may be.

Services Provided & Key Activities

Discretionary & Non-Discretionary PMS: An investor gives their portfolio in stocks, cash, or a combination of both. The minimum portfolio size under Discretionary/Non-Discretionary Funds Management is ₹50 lakhs as per current SEBI Regulations (the PMS provider may prescribe a higher threshold). Under Discretionary PMS, investment decisions are at the sole discretion of the PMS provider, in sync with the investor's objectives. Under Non-Discretionary PMS, investment decisions are taken at the discretion of the investor.

Investment Advisory Services: The client is advised on buy/sell decisions within the overall profile, without back-office responsibility for trade execution, custody, or accounting. The PMS provider acts solely as an Advisor, in a fiduciary capacity, maintaining an arm's-length relationship with its other activities.

  • Client on-boarding: KYC and AML compliance; execution of Power of Attorney; opening and funding of demat account; mapping the demat account with the Custodian.
  • Ongoing activities: Periodic statements as per PMS Regulations 2020; an audited annual account statement with all required details.
  • Fees and expenses: Charging and disclosure of fees in accordance with PMS Regulations.
  • Closure and termination: On termination of the PMS Agreement, securities and funds are transferred to the investor's verified bank/demat account.
  • Grievance redressal: Addressing investor queries, service requests and grievances in a time-bound manner.

Grievance Redressal Mechanism

It is mandatory for every PMS provider to register on SEBI SCORES (SEBI Complaint Redress System) — a centralised online complaint resolution system through which a complainant can raise a grievance and track its status.

1

Approach us directly

The name, address and phone number of our investor relations officer is provided in the PMS Disclosure Document, along with our grievance redressal and dispute mechanism.

2

Escalate to SEBI

File on SEBI SCORES. SEBI takes up the matter with us directly and follows up on your behalf.

Physical complaints may be sent to: Office of Investor Assistance and Education, Securities and Exchange Board of India, SEBI Bhavan, Plot No. C4-A, 'G' Block, Bandra-Kurla Complex, Bandra (E), Mumbai – 400 051.

Expectations from Investors

  • Check the registration status of the intermediary on the SEBI website before availing services.
  • Submit KYC documents and the application form in a timely manner, with signatures in the appropriate places and all requisite supporting documents.
  • Read the terms and conditions of the agreement carefully before signing.
  • Thoroughly study the Disclosure Document to understand the risks entailed by investing in PMS.
  • Answer the 'Risk Questionnaire' accurately and sincerely to help us assess your risk profile properly.
  • Study the quarterly statements we send, covering your portfolio's absolute and relative performance, its constituents, and its risk profile.
  • Provide complete details of your negative list of securities at the time of entering the PMS agreement, and every time thereafter for any changes.
  • Update us promptly of any change in your KYC documents or personal details, with the required proof.
