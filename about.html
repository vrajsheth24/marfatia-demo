Our Story

Built stone by stone, not overnight.

1994

Doors open in Vadodara

Marfatia acquires membership of the Vadodara Stock Exchange and starts business with five clients from a single office — built on a simple promise: answer the phone, know the client, mean what you say.

2005

NSE membership

NSE membership is acquired under Marfatia Stock Broking Ltd. in January 2005, with operations beginning that April — extending the desk beyond Vadodara Stock Exchange into cash, F&O and currency segments.

2016

Portfolio Management Services launch

A SEBI-registered PMS desk (INP000005117) opens for HNI and institutional clients wanting direct, customised equity portfolios.

Recent

Research Analyst registration & digital platform

A dedicated Research Analyst desk (INH000002178) and a modern trading & back-office platform bring three decades of market memory online.

Today

Full-service, still local

Broking, DP, PMS, MTF, SLB, IPO, Bonds, Insurance and Mutual Funds — served by a Vadodara-based team that still knows most clients by name.
