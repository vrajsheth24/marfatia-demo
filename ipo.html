What is an Initial Public Offering (IPO)?

An Initial Public Offering (IPO) is the first time a private company invites the public to buy its shares. By offering shares on the stock exchanges, the company transitions from a private structure to a publicly traded corporation, raising equity capital to fund expansion, pay down debt, or provide exit liquidity to early founders.

At Marfatia, we provide a completely paperless online bidding platform using the UPI block-mandate mechanism, ensuring your funds remain secure in your bank account until allotment is confirmed.
