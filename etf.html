Understand it

What is an Exchange Traded Fund (ETF)?

An Exchange Traded Fund (ETF) is an investment product that is listed and traded on a stock exchange, similar to shares. Each ETF typically tracks the performance of an underlying index, commodity, or basket of securities.

Instead of purchasing multiple individual securities, investors can gain diversified exposure through a single ETF unit. ETFs are held electronically in a Demat account and can be bought or sold during market hours at prevailing market prices.

Traded like a stock. ETFs combine mutual fund diversification with real-time exchange-traded liquidity.
