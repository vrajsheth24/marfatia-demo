HOW TO STAY SAFE

To ensure that you get world-class & seamless services from us and don’t fall prey to any fraudulent practices, here are a few important DOs & DON’Ts. Follow these instructions to keep your funds, securities, and sensitive trading information secure.

DOs — Recommended Investor Practices

Always follow these key practices when dealing in stock markets and maintaining your demat/trading accounts:

Trade Only with SEBI Registered Members

Trade only through Registered Members of the Exchange. Always check valid SEBI registration numbers before onboarding.

Read & Sign Voluntary Clauses Carefully

Ensure to read, understand and then sign the voluntary clauses, if any, agreed between you and the Member.

Register Mobile Number & Email ID

Register your mobile number and email ID with the Member to receive trade confirmation alerts/details of transactions directly from the Exchange by end of day.

Examine Scope of Power of Attorney (PoA)

Before granting Power of Attorney to anyone to operate your demat account, carefully examine the scope and implications of powers being granted.

Examine Authorised Person Credentials

Always examine the Authorised Person's credentials before Account Opening.

Verify Quarterly Account Statements

Always verify your Quarterly Statement of Account sent by MARFATIA.

Report Unauthorized Trading Immediately

Inform us of any irregularity or unauthorised trading activity observed by you immediately.

Regular Ledger Balance Verification

Check your ledger balance regularly on www.marfatia.net.

DON'Ts — Strictly Avoided Practices

Avoid these high-risk and unauthorized activities to keep your investments and credentials protected:

No Assured Return Schemes

Do not enter into assured returns arrangement. Fixed returns do not constitute guaranteed or assured returns in securities markets.

Avoid Unsolicited Tips & Rumours

Do not get carried away by luring advertisements, rumours, hot tips, explicit / implicit promise of returns, etc.

No Cash Transactions for Margin/Settlement

Do not make payments in cash / take any cash towards margins and settlement to / from the Member.

Never Trade Without Reading Risk Documents

Do not start trading before reading and understanding the Risk Disclosure Agreement prescribed by Stock Exchanges.

Protect Passwords & Login Credentials

Protect your login details and do not share login credentials with anyone. Password should be unique. Keep strong and complex passwords and change password at regular intervals.

Do Not Deal With Unregistered Entities

Do not deal with any unregistered intermediaries or unauthorized advisors.

Attention Investors — Essential Market Guidelines

  • Margin Pledge Rules: Stock Brokers can accept securities as margin from clients only by way of pledge in the depository system (w.e.f. September 1, 2020). Update your mobile number & email ID with your stock broker / depository participant to receive OTP directly from depository to create pledge.
  • Upfront Margin: Pay 20% or "VAR + ELM" whichever is higher as upfront margin of the transaction value to trade in cash market segment. Refer to Exchange FAQs (NSE/INSP/45191 & BSE 20200731-7).
  • Monthly CAS Verification: Check your Securities / MF / Bonds in the consolidated account statement (CAS) issued by NSDL/CDSL every month.
  • Aadhaar Linkage: Please link your Aadhaar with your Demat account as mandated by SEBI.
  • One-Time KYC: KYC is a one-time exercise while dealing in securities markets — once KYC is done through a SEBI registered intermediary (Broker, DP, Mutual Fund etc.), you need not undergo the same process again when you approach another intermediary.
  • IPO Subscription: No need to issue cheques by investors while subscribing to IPO. Just write the bank account number and sign in the application form to authorize your bank to make payment in case of allotment.

Grievance Redressal — SCORES & SMART ODR

If you have any grievance or complaint against the broker/DP that remains unresolved, you can file a complaint on official regulatory portals:

1. Filing Complaint on SEBI SCORES Portal

Filing complaint on SCORES is easy & quick:

  • Register on SCORES: Visit scores.gov.in and create an account.
  • Mandatory details: Name, PAN, Address, Mobile Number, Email ID.
  • Benefits: Effective communication and speedy redressal of grievances.

2. Filing Complaint on SMART ODR Portal

One of the prerequisites to file a complaint in SMART ODR is that the investor has to take up the complaint with the intermediary first before opting for ODR:

  1. Verify Prerequisites: Ensure your complaint has been raised with your broker/DP and remains unresolved beyond 21 days or response is unsatisfactory.
  2. Access Portal: Visit smartodr.in/login.
  3. File New Dispute: Choose participant type (Stock Broker / Depository Participant / PMS), category, upload contract notes / screenshots proof (up to 20MB), and submit.
