01
Understand it

What PMS is — in one minute.

Under the SEBI (Portfolio Managers) Regulations, 2020, a portfolio manager invests directly on your behalf. No pooled units — the portfolio is yours, the strategy is tailored, and the manager is personally answerable to you.

PMS is designed to provide total end-to-end customised and personalised services to HNI and institutional clients, enhancing wealth and returns through expert active fund management.

You own the securities. Stocks are bought in your demat account, so you always see exactly what you own.
