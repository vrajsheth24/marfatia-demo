Vision & Mission

Vision: Towards making the Indian Securities Market transparent, efficient, and investor friendly by providing a safe, reliable, transparent and trusted record-keeping platform for investors to hold and transfer securities in dematerialised form.

Mission:

  • To hold securities of investors in dematerialised form and facilitate their transfer, while ensuring safekeeping and protecting investor interest.
  • To provide timely and accurate information to investors regarding their holdings and transfers.
  • To provide the highest standards of investor education, awareness and timely service to enhance Investor Protection and awareness of Investor Rights.

Details of Business Transacted

A Depository is an organization that holds securities of investors in electronic form, and provides services to Exchanges, Clearing Corporations, Depository Participants (DPs), Issuers and Investors in both primary and secondary markets. The depository carries out its activities through its agents, known as Depository Participants (DP). Details are available at NSDL and CDSL.

Value-Added & Digital Services

  • Value Added Services: Basic Services Demat Account (BSDA), transposition-cum-dematerialization, linkages with the Clearing System, distribution of cash and non-cash corporate benefits (Bonus, Rights, IPOs, etc.), stock lending, demat of NSC/KVP, demat of warehouse receipts, and more.
  • Consolidated Account Statement (CAS): Issued 10 days from the end of the month (if there were transactions) or half-yearly (if no transactions).
  • Digitalization: E-account opening, online instructions for execution via e-DIS / Demat Gateway, e-CAS facility, online account closure, and monthly Statement of Transactions (SOT) — all available through CDSL's e-facilities.

Grievance Redressal Mechanism

An investor can lodge a complaint/grievance against the Depository/DP in the following ways:

  • Electronic mode: SEBI SCORES 2.0 (scores.sebi.gov.in) — a two-level review, first by the Designated Body and then by SEBI; the Depository's own web portal (e.g. CDSL grievances); or email to complaints@cdslindia.com.
  • Offline mode: Complaints lodged directly with the Depository shall be resolved within 21 days.

Online Dispute Resolution (ODR)

If not satisfied with the resolution provided by the DP or other market participants, you may file your complaint on the SMART ODR platform for resolution through online conciliation or arbitration.

1

Approach the Market Participant

Reach out to us first for redressal of your complaint.

2

Escalate to SEBI SCORES or SMARTODR

If not satisfied, escalate on SEBI SCORES, or alternatively file on SMARTODR for resolution through online conciliation and arbitration.

3

Conciliation, then Arbitration

The relevant MII reviews the matter and endeavours to resolve it within 21 days. If unresolved, conciliation is attempted (21 days, extendable by 10). If still unresolved, arbitration follows, concluded within 30 days (extendable by 30).

Termination of a Depository Participant

Depositories terminate a participant's participation if it no longer meets eligibility criteria or on other grounds mentioned in the bye-laws, such as suspension by the Stock Exchanges, or if the participant surrenders participation voluntarily. In either case, the client has the right to transfer all securities to any other Participant of choice, without charges, within 30 days of intimation by letter/email.
