Understand it

What are Bonds?

A Bond is a fixed-income investment where investors lend money to a government, public sector undertaking, or corporate entity for a specified period. In return, the issuer pays periodic interest (coupon) and repays the principal amount at maturity.

Bonds are considered an important investment option for individuals seeking portfolio diversification, stable income, and relatively lower risk compared to equity investments.

Balance your portfolio. Bonds offer predictable income and lower volatility alongside your equity holdings.
