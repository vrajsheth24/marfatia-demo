Understand it

What is Equity?

Equity represents ownership in a company. By purchasing shares of a listed company, investors become partial owners and participate in the company's growth as well as associated market risks.

Investors may benefit through potential capital appreciation, dividend income (if declared), participation in corporate actions, and long-term wealth creation — all through a secure, technology-driven trading platform.

Own a piece of the business. Equity investing lets you participate directly in a listed company's growth and performance.

Benefits of Equity Investing
