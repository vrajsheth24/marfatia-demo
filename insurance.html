Risk Cover

Strategic Risk Coverage Advisory

Insurance is not an investment; it is a critical tool for risk management. Having the right coverage preserves your family's financial stability and corporate balance sheets from unforeseen health crises, accidents, or asset losses.

At Marfatia, we act as corporate insurance advisors, comparing policies across life, health, and general insurance providers to select coverages matching your requirements without insurer bias.

Exclusions

Essential policy terms to understand upfront.

Exclusions determine whether claims are paid. We highlight crucial limits before policy commitment.

Waiting Period

Pre-existing Diseases

2 - 4 Years Industry standard

Hospitalization for existing medical issues (e.g. diabetes) is covered only after the specified waiting period.

Sub-Limits

Room Rent & Treatments

Capped Refer to policy schedule

Insurers cap room rent charges (typically 1% of sum insured) or set limits on specific surgical procedures.

Cashless Network

Cashless Hospital Network

Varies Insurer portal listing

Cashless settlement is available only at hospitals in the insurer's network. Out-of-network claims require reimbursement.

Free Look Period

15 Days To cancel with full refund

You can cancel a newly issued policy within 15 days if you find the terms unsatisfactory.
