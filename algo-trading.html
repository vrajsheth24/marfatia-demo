Understand it

What is Algorithmic Trading?

Algorithmic trading is the automated placement, modification, and execution of orders based on predefined trading rules. These rules are converted into systematic trading strategies and executed through approved trading infrastructure.

Strategies can be built using factors such as price movement, volume, technical indicators, volatility, open interest, time-based conditions, risk limits and portfolio exposure — enabling faster, rule-driven decision-making.

Rules, not guesswork. Algo trading converts defined market logic into systematic, repeatable execution.
